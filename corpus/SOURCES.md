## Rebuilding

The SQAM clips come from `TECH3253_SQAM_FLAC` (167.4 MB zip of the CD's 70
FLAC tracks, 44.1 kHz/16-bit), download linked from
https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/sqamcd (served from
https://qc.ebu.io/testmaterials/523/1/download/). Each clip is the first 10 s
of the track, converted with
`ffmpeg -i NN.flac -t 10 -c:a pcm_s16le corpus/<set>/sqamNN_<name>.wav`.
Tracks 49–52 are mono; the rest are stereo.
